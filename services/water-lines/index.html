Your water line brings every drop of water into your home or business — when it leaks or fails, everything suffers. We diagnose water line problems properly, then repair or replace the line so your pressure and water quality are back to normal.

Shafa Plumbing Services is based in Scarborough, and we provide water line repair and replacement across Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, East York, Markham, Richmond Hill, Pickering, Ajax, Oshawa and Mississauga.
