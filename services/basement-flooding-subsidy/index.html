Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy in Toronto

Get up to $6,650 back from the City of Toronto for a backwater valve, sump pump and battery backup — installed by a licensed plumber, across Toronto & the GTA.

One heavy storm can push sewage up through your basement floor drain — or flood the basement with groundwater. A backwater valve and a sump pump stop both, and since May 1, 2026 the City of Toronto’s Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program covers 80% of the invoiced cost, up to $6,650 per property.

Shafa Plumbing Services is based in Scarborough, and we install backwater valves and sump pumps across Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, East York, Markham, Richmond Hill, Pickering, Ajax and Oshawa. Every job starts with finding the real problem, and all our work is backed by a 1-year warranty — Licence No. P34765.

What the subsidy covers

  • ✓ Backwater valve — up to $1,600 per device (max 2)
  • ✓ Sump pump — up to $2,250
  • ✓ Battery backup — up to $300
  • ✓ Weeping-tile severance & capping — up to $400
  • ✓ Home plumbing assessment — up to $500

How it works: the City covers 80% of the invoiced cost, up to the amounts above. The work must be done by a contractor holding a valid City of Toronto licence in an eligible category — ask us to confirm before work starts. Keep your paid invoice and proof of payment: you apply to the City after the work is done, and the City decides the claim. Read our full guide: Basement Flooding Protection in Toronto.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I get back from the subsidy?

The City of Toronto program covers 80% of the invoiced cost, up to $6,650 per property across eligible items — including a backwater valve, sump pump and battery backup.

Who can apply for the subsidy?

Homeowners in the City of Toronto — that includes Scarborough, Etobicoke and North York. The City decides each claim.

Does the work have to be done by a licensed contractor?

Yes — the work must be done by a contractor holding a valid City of Toronto licence in an eligible category. Ask us to confirm before work starts.

How do I claim the money?

You apply to the City after the work is done. Keep your paid invoice and proof of payment — we give you the paperwork from the job for your claim.

How do I get started?

Call 647-710-3376 or book online. We will look at your basement, explain what qualifies, and confirm your price before any work begins.
