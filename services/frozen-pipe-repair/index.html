Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if a pipe freezes?

If a pipe has burst, turn off your main water shut-off valve and call us at 647-710-3376. Never use an open flame to thaw a pipe.

Can a frozen pipe burst?

Yes. Ice expands as it freezes and can split the pipe — and the leak often only shows once the pipe thaws. Frozen pipes should be checked quickly.

How can I stop pipes from freezing?

Keep the heat on, insulate pipes in cold spots like garages and crawl spaces, and let faucets drip a little on very cold nights.
