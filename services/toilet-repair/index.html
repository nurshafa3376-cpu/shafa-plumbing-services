Frequently Asked Questions

What toilet problems do you fix?

Running toilets, leaks at the base, clogs, weak flushing and toilets that will not flush at all.

Why does my toilet keep running?

Usually a worn flapper or fill valve inside the tank. We check the cause first and confirm your price before any work begins.

Can a running toilet raise my water bill?

Yes — a toilet that runs or leaks wastes water all day and night. It is one of the most common causes of a surprise high water bill.

Do you install new toilets?

Yes — we take out the old toilet and install the new one with a new wax seal and supply hose, and check everything for leaks.

Is the work covered by a warranty?

Yes — our work is covered by a 1-year warranty, and we are licensed (Licence P34765) and insured.
