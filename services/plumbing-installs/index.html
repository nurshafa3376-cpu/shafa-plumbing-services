New fixture or new appliance? A plumbing install done properly the first time helps prevent leaks, callbacks and water damage later. We install toilets, faucets, sinks, showers, water heaters and sump pumps, and hook up appliances like washers — fitted, connected and tested before we leave.

Shafa Plumbing Services is based in Scarborough, and we provide plumbing installs across Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, East York, Markham, Richmond Hill, Pickering, Ajax, Oshawa and Mississauga.
