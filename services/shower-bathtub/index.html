A leaking shower or tub isn't just annoying — water getting behind tiles and under floors causes real damage over time. We repair shower and bathtub plumbing properly: leaks, drips, valves, drains and fixtures, in homes and businesses across the GTA.

Shafa Plumbing Services is based in Scarborough, and we provide shower and bathtub repair across Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, East York, Markham, Richmond Hill, Pickering, Ajax, Oshawa and Mississauga.
